How to play
  1. Two players take turns. Red goes first, then Gold.
  2. On your turn, tap a column. Your disc drops to the lowest empty space.
  3. Get four of your discs in a row (across, up and down, or diagonal) to win.
  4. If all 42 spaces fill up with no line of four, it's a draw.
  5. Share the game code. Your friend enters it to take the other seat. More visitors can watch.
